Pick your side

Two sides, one war-torn world. This is the fantasy each faction sells, what its lands look like, and what Forever changes for it.

Alliance

Honour, duty and the light

The shield of civilisation: old kingdoms that set aside their rivalries to defend their cities, farms and libraries from whatever crawls out of the dark. It is a fantasy of knights, craftsmen and ancient forest wardens standing shoulder to shoulder.

Look and feel
Royal blue and burnished gold. Stone keeps, cathedral light, heraldic banners, polished plate, warm hearths and green countryside.
Homelands
Stormwind for Humans, Ironforge for Dwarves and Gnomes, Darnassus for Night Elves.
You will like it if
You want a clear heroic cause, tidy heraldry, and the feeling of defending something worth keeping.

New in Forever: Dwarves can be Shaman, which gives the Alliance its first Shaman. High Order Skyborne join as the faction's only new race, with Mage access.

Horde

Strength, survival and found family

A coalition of outcasts: orcs, trolls, tauren and the free-willed dead, all pushed to the edges of the world who decided to survive on their own terms. It is a fantasy of honour earned instead of inherited, and of loyalty over pedigree.

Look and feel
Crimson and blackened iron. Spikes, hide, bone, war drums, ritual paint, red rock, scorched plains and campfires.
Homelands
Orgrimmar for Orcs and Trolls, Thunder Bluff for Tauren, the Undercity for the Undead (the Forsaken).
You will like it if
You love underdog stories, primal and tribal aesthetics, and a bit of moral grey.

New in Forever: Undead can be Paladins, which gives the Horde its first Paladin. Windshaper Skyborne join with Shaman access.

Where and when Forever happens

Forever is a separate branch of WoW built on the original 2004 game. It sits in the first year of the world, after the events of the Forsaken Kingdom campaign and before Molten Core, and it does not share continuity with modern WoW. The level cap stays at 60 and new content arrives as stories, zones, dungeons and raids instead of new level ranges. It is included with a WoW subscription, except the Skyborne race, which needs the Skyborne Heroic Pack or higher.

The ten peoples

Every race has four racials in Forever: two actives (one combat cooldown, one role-play or utility) and two passives. A gold star on a class marks a combination that is new to Forever. Racial numbers come from beta-era guides and may shift.

Nine classes

No new classes, but Blizzard has reworked every class and talent tree so each of the 27 specs has a place in dungeons or raids. Each card shows who can play it.
